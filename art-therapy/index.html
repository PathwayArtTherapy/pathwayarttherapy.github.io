Art therapy in Australia

Art therapy for when words don’t come easily

Neuro-affirming online art therapy for children, teens and young adults living in Australia. Individual and small group sessions, paid privately or through NDIS. No art experience needed.

Sound familiar?

What families have come to me with

  • Big emotions that feel impossible to name or explain
  • Meltdowns or shutdowns that leave everyone exhausted
  • Selective mutism, or a child who goes quiet in certain settings
  • Anxiety around social situations, school or changes in routine
  • Feeling like talk-based support hasn’t quite landed
  • Wanting connection and regulation tools that don’t feel clinical

If any of this sounds familiar, you’re in the right place.

Why art therapy

A way in that doesn’t rely on words

Art therapy gives young people a way to process and regulate without having to explain themselves in words. That’s often exactly what talk-based approaches ask of them, and where they get stuck.

Your child doesn’t need to be good at art. They don’t need the right words. Curiosity and a safe space to explore are enough.

I’m Lou Lou, and I’ve worked in trauma-informed therapeutic roles with neurodivergent children and teens for more than eight years.

  • Masters-trained art therapist
  • Professional Member of ANZACATA
  • Sessions fully online, anywhere in Australia

What’s on offer

Ways to work together

One-to-one or in a small group, paid privately or funded through NDIS.

  • Individual sessions

    One-to-one online sessions, shaped around your child rather than a fixed curriculum. 50 minutes privately, or 45 minutes with NDIS funding.

  • Small groups

    Structured, neuro-affirming 90-minute groups of 2 to 6 children, ages 9 to 13. Social groups

  • NDIS funding

    Plan-managed and self-managed plans accepted, with clear, itemised invoices. NDIS FAQs

  • Fully online

    Join from home, wherever you are in Australia. No travel, no waiting rooms.

Living outside Australia? Art therapy is for people living in Australia. Wherever else you are, you’re very welcome to join a creative wellbeing session instead. They’re gentle, therapeutic creative sessions, shaped by my art therapy training, and fully online.

How to get started

Three gentle steps

Go at whatever pace feels right. If you’re ready, you can skip the call and book a first session.

  1. 1

    Free intro call

    Free30 minutesOptional

    A relaxed chat to see if we’re a good fit and to answer your questions. We can’t be everyone’s cup of tea, and that’s ok!

  2. 2

    First session

    45–50 minutesOnline

    A gentle place to land. We focus on comfort and trust, often with a simple, grounding art activity, so your child gets a feel for what sessions are like.

  3. 3

    Ongoing sessions

    45–50 minutesOnlineYour pace

    Most families start weekly to build trust and routine. Then we adjust the pace together, based on what’s working.
