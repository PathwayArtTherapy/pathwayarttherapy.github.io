Is this for you?

Who is this for?

This tends to be useful if you’re:

  • New to delivering art therapy or creative modalities online, and unsure how techniques translate to a screen
  • Running (or planning) online group sessions and finding engagement harder to hold than in person
  • Working with NDIS clients online and wanting practical, tested approaches rather than theory
  • Looking for a sounding board on cases or format, without a formal supervision arrangement
  • Wanting to build confidence in an area most training doesn’t cover in depth

What this looks like

Two ways to work together

Book a single session around a specific challenge, or set up an ongoing arrangement.

  • 1:1 consultation sessions

    Bring a case, a format challenge, or a “how would you handle this online” question. We focus on the particular client, group or challenge you’re working through.

  • Professional development sessions

    Practical training on adapting art therapy techniques, engagement strategies and group facilitation for online delivery. More structured, and focused on building a specific skill.

Good to know: these are consultation and professional development sessions, rather than clinical supervision. The PD FAQs cover supervision and CPD in more detail.
