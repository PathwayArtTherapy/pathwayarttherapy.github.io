Professional development

Professional development FAQs

Common questions about consultation and professional development sessions for art therapists and allied health professionals working online.

Is this clinical supervision?

Not at the moment. I’m a Professional Member of ANZACATA working toward my Supervision qualification. What I offer now is consultation and professional development: practical, experience-led sessions on delivering art therapy and creative modalities online.

If you’re looking for a registered supervisor, I’m happy to point you toward one while I complete this qualification.

What’s the difference between consultation and professional development sessions?

Consultation is case- or format-specific. Bring a particular client, group or challenge you’re working through.

Professional development sessions are more structured, and focus on building a specific skill, such as adapting a technique for online delivery or running engaged online groups.

Who is this for?

Art therapists and allied health professionals delivering creative modalities online, particularly if you’re:

  • newer to online delivery
  • working with NDIS clients online and wanting practical, tested approaches rather than theory.

See who it tends to help

Are sessions one-off or ongoing?

Either. Some people book a single session around a specific challenge. Others set up a regular, ongoing arrangement.

Do sessions count toward CPD?

These sessions aren’t formal supervision hours. What I offer is consultation and professional development, rather than accredited supervision.

Many professional bodies do allow peer consultation and PD activities to be self-logged as CPD, so you’re welcome to include our sessions in your own record. I’d recommend checking your specific requirements with your registering body to confirm.

How much do sessions cost?

Sessions are online, run for 50 minutes and cost AUD $100 per session.

How do I book?

Sessions are booked by enquiry rather than instant online booking. Send me a message and I’ll reply directly to find a time.

Still have a question?

Send me a message about your work and what you’d like to focus on. I’ll reply directly.
