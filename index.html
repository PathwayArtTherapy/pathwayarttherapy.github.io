Some of the things people bring to sessions

  • Big emotions that are hard to name
  • Anxiety around social situations, school or change
  • Going quiet, shutting down or selective mutism
  • Meltdowns that leave everyone exhausted
  • Perfectionism, and when things don’t go the “right” way
  • Big life changes, like moving to a new country

You don’t need to be an artist. You don’t need the right words. Curiosity is enough.

Hi, I’m Lou Lou

Grounded, curious and human

I’m a creative wellbeing practitioner and Masters-trained art therapist. I’ve spent more than eight years in trauma-informed roles with neurodivergent children and teens.

In 2024 I left Australia to live overseas, so I know first-hand how change can shake your sense of self. I meet you where you are, without judgement and without pressure.

  • Professional Member of ANZACATA
  • Individual and group sessions, fully online

How to get started

Three gentle steps

Go at whatever pace feels right. If you’re ready, you can skip straight to a first session.

  1. 1

    Free intro call

    Free30 minutesOptional

    A relaxed chat to see if we’re a good fit and to answer your questions. We can’t be everyone’s cup of tea, and that’s ok!

  2. 2

    First session

    45–50 minutesOnline

    A gentle place to land. We’ll often try a simple, grounding art activity, so you get a feel for what sessions are like.

  3. 3

    Ongoing sessions

    45–50 minutesOnlineYour pace

    Most people start weekly to build trust and routine. Together we’ll find a rhythm that suits you.
