Booking your first session

  1. 1

    Choose a time

    Pick any time that suits you in the booking calendar. It will show as 30 minutes, and that’s fine.

  2. 2

    Write “First session” in the notes

    When you book, add “First session” in the notes box. If you’re using NDIS funding, mention that too.

  3. 3

    I’ll hold the full hour

    I’ll extend your booking to a full session and email you to confirm, along with a short form to fill in before we meet.

These are non-clinical, and aren’t a substitute for counselling, psychotherapy or mental health treatment.
