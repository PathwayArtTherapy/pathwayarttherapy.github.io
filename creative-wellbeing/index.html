What it is

What is creative wellbeing?

Creative wellbeing is a gentle practice that uses creative processes to support your emotional and mental wellbeing.

It isn’t about being artistic or making something to show. Creativity becomes a way to slow down, reflect, express and make sense of what you’re living through, especially when words feel limited or overused.

It’s for people who want support without labels, pressure or performance, and a more human, spacious way to care for themselves.

What matters most

  • Process over outcome. What you make matters less than how it feels to make it.
  • Curiosity over fixing. Nothing about you needs fixing. We explore with interest.
  • Presence over productivity. Time to simply be here, without a to-do list.

Why creative wellbeing? My art therapy registration is Australian, so outside Australia I offer this work as creative wellbeing. Sessions are shaped by my art therapy training and can feel deeply therapeutic, but they’re non-clinical. They aren’t counselling, psychotherapy, mental health treatment or crisis support.

If you need urgent help, please contact your local emergency services.

Art therapy in Australia

What to expect

What a session might look like

Sessions are online, 50 minutes long, and shaped around you or your group. A session may include:

  • A gentle check-in
  • A creative prompt to explore, such as writing, drawing, collage, movement, metaphor or working with objects
  • Time to reflect, quietly or with some guidance
  • Optional conversation about what came up
  • A grounding close, to help you return to your day

There’s no right or wrong way to take part, and sharing is always optional.

Who it’s for

Sessions for people living outside Australia

For anyone wanting space to explore their wellbeing at their own pace, one-to-one or in a group.

  • Adults and professionals

    Reflective support for stress, burnout and busy working lives.

  • People living abroad

    Including partners of travelling professionals. Space to process a move, time apart and adjusting to a new culture.

  • People living with chronic pain

    A gentle way to reconnect with yourself when pain takes up a lot of room.

  • Young people and families

    Creative sessions for children, teens and families, including neurodivergent young people.

  • Groups and communities

    Connection, shared reflection and collective care. See social groups.

How it can help

What sessions may support

  • Settling your emotions and your nervous system during stress, distance or overwhelm
  • Gentle self-reflection and insight, without pressure or expectation
  • Reconnecting with yourself when you’re living with chronic pain, navigating time apart or feeling stuck
  • Finding steadiness within change, uncertainty or complex life dynamics
  • Processing transitions, like moving abroad or adjusting to a new culture
  • Managing work and life stress, and helping to prevent burnout

No creative experience needed. Curiosity is enough, and even that can arrive slowly.
