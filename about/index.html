My story

Hi, I’m Lou Lou

I’m a creative wellbeing practitioner and Masters-trained art therapist. I work online with people in Australia and around the world, especially through complex life challenges.

Book a free intro call Ways to work with me

Who I am

What shapes my work

When we’re given space to pause, create and connect, even briefly, something meaningful can shift. That belief sits at the heart of everything I do.

I especially love supporting:

  • Partners of travelling professionals
  • Adults navigating chronic pain
  • Neurodivergent children and teens
  • People who are non-verbal or experience selective mutism

Lived experience, as well as training

I love travel, creative expression and connecting across cultures. I’m a partner, step-parent, sister and daughter, and I’ve experienced loss and grief.

In 2024 I left Australia to live overseas. That change keeps expanding how I understand identity and belonging.

All of this shapes how I show up for you: grounded, curious and human.

My approach

I meet you where you are

No judgement, no teaching and no advice-giving.

This work is collaborative. Along the way we may find moments that weren’t planned, and together we shape a space for safety, curiosity and self-discovery.

You choose what you share, and how deeply you go.

You do not need to be an artist. You do not need the right words. Curiosity is enough.

Scope of my work

Where you live shapes the kind of session

Every session is online. My art therapy registration applies in Australia, so what I offer depends on where you live.

  • Living in Australia

    Art therapy, privately or through plan-managed or self-managed NDIS. Art therapy in Australia

  • Living anywhere else

    Creative wellbeing sessions, using simple, accessible art-based practices. Creative wellbeing

Scope of practice for creative wellbeing. My art therapy registration is Australian, so outside Australia I offer this work as creative wellbeing. Sessions are shaped by my art therapy training and can feel deeply therapeutic, but they’re non-clinical. They aren’t counselling, psychotherapy, mental health treatment or crisis support.

They’re for people who want care without being pathologised, and who are drawn to creativity as a way of understanding themselves, even if they don’t feel “creative”.

An invitation

You don’t need a clear goal, or to know what you want to work on. If this feels like the space you’ve been looking for, you’re welcome to begin.
