Art therapy is offered to people living in Australia by Lou Lou Rodrigues, a Professional Member of ANZACATA. Outside Australia, Lou Lou offers creative wellbeing sessions and groups, shaped by her art therapy training. These are non-clinical, and aren’t a substitute for counselling, psychotherapy or mental health treatment.
